Cartridge builder

Drag the box over the area you ride. A cartridge holds that region's map, its routing tiles and its searchable places, and the app works with no signal once it is in. Building one is a server job — this page gives you the command.
Shown in the app under Settings › Map pack.
The Geofabrik extract your box sits in. Downloaded once and reused.

Needs osmium, docker and go-pmtiles. The map comes out of

      about a minute for a metro area.
